From A-level Maths and Further Maths: a **vector** is a list of numbers, such as

$$\mathbf{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix}$$

You add two vectors entry by entry, and multiplying by a number scales every entry. In 2D or 3D you can picture a vector as an arrow, but nothing stops a vector having a million entries. That is how a picture becomes a vector: see [an image as a vector](c:image-as-vector).

On the slides, vectors are usually written in plain letters like $x_n$ rather than bold, because everything is a vector there.
