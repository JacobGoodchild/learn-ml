title: The exponential function
summary: e to the power x. It grows fast for positive x and dies away towards 0 for negative x.
colour: calculus

---

From A-level Maths: $e^x$, where $e \approx 2.718$. It is always positive. As $x$ becomes very negative, $e^x$ shrinks towards 0 very quickly. Its inverse is the natural log, $\ln x$, which the slides write as $\log x$.

The bell curve uses $e^{-z^2/2}$: squaring makes the exponent negative on both sides of the middle, so the curve falls away symmetrically and very fast. That is why values far from the mean are so rare.
