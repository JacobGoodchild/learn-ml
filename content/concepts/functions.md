From A-level: $f(x)$ is the output of the function $f$ for the input $x$. Nothing stops the input being a whole vector, such as a picture, or the output being a vector too.

On the slides, a dot in brackets, as in $\mu_n(\cdot)$ or $s_n(\cdot)$, means "the function itself, whatever its input". So "choose the best $\mu_n(\cdot)$" means "choose the best *function*", not the best single number. Choosing a whole function sounds impossible, but in practice the function is a neural network, and choosing it means choosing the network's settings.
